### <span color="purple">**Verset 1**</span>
## <span color="green">**بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِیمِ عَمَّ یَتَسَاۤءَلُونَ<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*'Amma Yatasaa-aloon*</span>
<span color="brown">Sur quoi s'interrogent-ils mutuellement?</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 2**</span>
## <span color="green">**عَنِ ٱلنَّبَإِ ٱلۡعَظِیمِ<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*'Anin-nabaa-il 'azeem*</span>
<span color="brown">Sur la grande nouvelle,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 3**</span>
## <span color="green">**ٱلَّذِی هُمۡ فِیهِ مُخۡتَلِفُونَ<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Allazi hum feehi mukh talifoon*</span>
<span color="brown">à propos de laquelle ils divergent.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 4**</span>
## <span color="green">**كَلَّا سَیَعۡلَمُونَ<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Kallaa sa y'alamoon*</span>
<span color="brown">Eh bien non! Ils sauront bientôt.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 5**</span>
## <span color="green">**ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَیَعۡلَمُونَ<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Thumma kallaa sa y'alamoon*</span>
<span color="brown">Encore une fois, non! Ils sauront bientôt.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 6**</span>
## <span color="green">**أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَلِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِهَـٰدࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Alam naj'alil arda mihaa da*</span>
<span color="brown">N'avons-Nous pas fait de la terre une couche?</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 7**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَٱلۡجِبَالَ أَوۡتَادࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wal jibaala au taada*</span>
<span color="brown">et (placé) les montagnes comme des piquets?</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 8**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَخَلَقۡنَـٰكُمۡ أَزۡوَ ٰ⁠جࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa khalaq naakum azwaaja*</span>
<span color="brown">Nous vous avons créés en couples,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 9**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَجَعَلۡنَا نَوۡمَكُمۡ سُبَاتࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Waja'alnan naumakum subata*</span>
<span color="brown">et désigné votre sommeil pour votre repos,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 10**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلَّیۡلَ لِبَاسࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Waja'alnal laila libasa*</span>
<span color="brown">et fait de la nuit un vêtement,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 11**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلنَّهَارَ مَعَاشࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Waja'alnan nahara ma 'aasha*</span>
<span color="brown">et assigné le jour pour les affaires de la vie,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 12**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَبَنَیۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمۡ سَبۡعࣰا شِدَادࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa banaina fauqakum sab 'an shi daada*</span>
<span color="brown">et construit au-dessus de vous sept (cieux) renforcés,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 13**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَجَعَلۡنَا سِرَاجࣰا وَهَّاجࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Waja'alna siraajaw wah haaja*</span>
<span color="brown">et [y] avons placé une lampe (le soleil) très ardente,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 14**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمُعۡصِرَ ٰ⁠تِ مَاۤءࣰ ثَجَّاجࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa anzalna minal m'usiraati maa-an saj-jaaja*</span>
<span color="brown">et fait descendre des nuées une eau abondante</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 15**</span>
## <span color="green">**لِّنُخۡرِجَ بِهِۦ حَبࣰّا وَنَبَاتࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Linukh rija bihee habbaw wana baata*</span>
<span color="brown">pour faire pousser par elle grains et plantes</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 16**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَجَنَّـٰتٍ أَلۡفَافًا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa jan naatin alfafa*</span>
<span color="brown">et jardins luxuriants.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 17**</span>
## <span color="green">**إِنَّ یَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ كَانَ مِیقَـٰتࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Inna yaumal-fasli kana miqaata*</span>
<span color="brown">Le Jour de la Décision [du Jugement] a son terme fixé.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 18**</span>
## <span color="green">**یَوۡمَ یُنفَخُ فِی ٱلصُّورِ فَتَأۡتُونَ أَفۡوَاجࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Yauma yun fakhu fis-soori fataa toona afwaaja*</span>
<span color="brown">Le jour où l'on soufflera dans la Trompe, vous viendrez par troupes,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 19**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَفُتِحَتِ ٱلسَّمَاۤءُ فَكَانَتۡ أَبۡوَ ٰ⁠بࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa futiha tis samaa-u fakaanat abwaaba*</span>
<span color="brown">et le ciel sera ouvert et [présentera] des portes,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 20**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَسُیِّرَتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ فَكَانَتۡ سَرَابًا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa suyyi raatil jibaalu fa kaanat saraaba*</span>
<span color="brown">et les montagnes seront mises en marche et deviendront un mirage.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 21**</span>
## <span color="green">**إِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ كَانَتۡ مِرۡصَادࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Inna jahan nama kaanat mirsaada*</span>
<span color="brown">L'Enfer demeure aux aguets,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 22**</span>
## <span color="green">**لِّلطَّـٰغِینَ مَـَٔابࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Lit taa gheena ma aaba*</span>
<span color="brown">refuge pour les transgresseurs.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 23**</span>
## <span color="green">**لَّـٰبِثِینَ فِیهَاۤ أَحۡقَابࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Laa bitheena feehaa ahqaaba*</span>
<span color="brown">Ils y demeureront pendant des siècles successifs.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 24**</span>
## <span color="green">**لَّا یَذُوقُونَ فِیهَا بَرۡدࣰا وَلَا شَرَابًا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Laa ya zooqoona feeha bar daw walaa sharaaba*</span>
<span color="brown">Ils n'y goûteront ni fraîcheur ni breuvage,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 25**</span>
## <span color="green">**إِلَّا حَمِیمࣰا وَغَسَّاقࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Illa hamee maw-wa ghas saaqa*</span>
<span color="brown">Hormis une eau bouillante et un pus</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 26**</span>
## <span color="green">**جَزَاۤءࣰ وِفَاقًا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Jazaa-aw wi faaqa*</span>
<span color="brown">comme rétribution équitable.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 27**</span>
## <span color="green">**إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُوا۟ لَا یَرۡجُونَ حِسَابࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Innahum kaanu laa yarjoona hisaaba*</span>
<span color="brown">Car ils ne s'attendaient pas à rendre compte,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 28**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَكَذَّبُوا۟ بِـَٔایَـٰتِنَا كِذَّابࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa kazzabu bi aayaa tina kizzaba*</span>
<span color="brown">et traitaient de mensonges, continuellement, Nos versets,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 29**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَكُلَّ شَیۡءٍ أَحۡصَیۡنَـٰهُ كِتَـٰبࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa kulla shai-in ahsai naahu kitaa ba*</span>
<span color="brown">alors que Nous avons dénombré toutes choses en écrit.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 30**</span>
## <span color="green">**فَذُوقُوا۟ فَلَن نَّزِیدَكُمۡ إِلَّا عَذَابًا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Fa zooqoo falan-nazee dakum ill-laa azaaba*</span>
<span color="brown">Goûtez-donc. Nous n'augmenterons pour vous que le châtiment!</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 31**</span>
## <span color="green">**إِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِینَ مَفَازًا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Inna lil mutta qeena mafaaza*</span>
<span color="brown">Pour les pieux ce sera une réussite:</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 32**</span>
## <span color="green">**حَدَاۤىِٕقَ وَأَعۡنَـٰبࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Hadaa-iqa wa a'anaa ba*</span>
<span color="brown">jardins et vignes,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 33**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَكَوَاعِبَ أَتۡرَابࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa kaawa 'iba at raaba*</span>
<span color="brown">et des (belles) aux seins arrondis, d'une égale jeunesse,</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 34**</span>
## <span color="green">**وَكَأۡسࣰا دِهَاقࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Wa ka'san di haaqa*</span>
<span color="brown">et coupes débordantes.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 35**</span>
## <span color="green">**لَّا یَسۡمَعُونَ فِیهَا لَغۡوࣰا وَلَا كِذَّ ٰ⁠بࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Laa yasma'oona fiha lagh waw walaa kizzaba*</span>
<span color="brown">Ils n'y entendront ni futilités ni mensonges.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 36**</span>
## <span color="green">**جَزَاۤءࣰ مِّن رَّبِّكَ عَطَاۤءً حِسَابࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Jazaa-am mir-rabbika ataa-an hisaaba*</span>
<span color="brown">A titre de récompense de ton Seigneur et à titre de don abondant</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 37**</span>
## <span color="green">**رَّبِّ ٱلسَّمَـٰوَ ٰ⁠تِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَیۡنَهُمَا ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنِۖ لَا یَمۡلِكُونَ مِنۡهُ خِطَابࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Rabbis samaa waati wal ardi wa maa baina humar rahmaani laa yam likoona minhu khi taaba*</span>
<span color="brown">du Seigneur des cieux et de la terre et de ce qui existe entre eux, le Tout Miséricordieux; ils n'osent nullement Lui adresser la parole.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 38**</span>
## <span color="green">**یَوۡمَ یَقُومُ ٱلرُّوحُ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰۤىِٕكَةُ صَفࣰّاۖ لَّا یَتَكَلَّمُونَ إِلَّا مَنۡ أَذِنَ لَهُ ٱلرَّحۡمَـٰنُ وَقَالَ صَوَابࣰا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Yauma yaqoo mur roohu wal malaa-ikatu saf-fal laa yata kalla moona illa man azina lahur rahmaanu wa qaala sawaaba*</span>
<span color="brown">Le jour où l'Esprit et les Anges se dresseront en rangs, nul ne saura parler, sauf celui à qui le Tout Miséricordieux aura accordé la permission, et qui dira la vérité.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 39**</span>
## <span color="green">**ذَ ٰ⁠لِكَ ٱلۡیَوۡمُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ فَمَن شَاۤءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ مَـَٔابًا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*Zaalikal yaumul haqqu faman shaa-at ta khaaza ill-laa rabbihi ma-aaba*</span>
<span color="brown">Ce jour-là est inéluctable. Que celui qui veut prenne donc refuge auprès de son Seigneur.</span>
---
### <span color="purple">**Verset 40**</span>
## <span color="green">**إِنَّاۤ أَنذَرۡنَـٰكُمۡ عَذَابࣰا قَرِیبࣰا یَوۡمَ یَنظُرُ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مَا قَدَّمَتۡ یَدَاهُ وَیَقُولُ ٱلۡكَافِرُ یَـٰلَیۡتَنِی كُنتُ تُرَ ٰ⁠بَۢا<br>**</span>
<span color="blue">*In naa anzar naakum azaaban qareebaiy-yauma yan zurul marr-u maa qaddamat yadaahu wa ya qoolul-kaafiru yaa lai tanee kuntu turaaba*</span>
<span color="brown">Nous vous avons avertis d'un châtiment bien proche, le jour où l'homme verra ce que ses deux mains ont préparé; et l'infidèle dira: «Hélas pour moi! Comme j'aurais aimé n'être que poussière».</span>
